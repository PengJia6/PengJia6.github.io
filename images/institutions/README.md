# Institution marks used in the career sections

- `xjtu.png`: Xi'an Jiaotong University red seal, downloaded unchanged from the university's [visual identity download page](https://vi.xjtu.edu.cn/ksxz/ksxz1.htm). Asset: https://vi.xjtu.edu.cn/img/a1-3jdxhred.png
- `cqu.png`: Chongqing University seal, downloaded unchanged from the public [CQU-ADHRI-Lab profile](https://github.com/CQU-ADHRI-Lab). Asset: https://avatars.githubusercontent.com/u/150583760?v=4&s=256. The design was checked against the university's [official homepage wordmark](https://www.cqu.edu.cn/images/logo1.png).
- The hospital row uses the existing Font Awesome `fa-hospital` symbol as an illustrative medical icon, not an official hospital emblem. The official hospital identity downloads could not be retrieved during this update.

Retrieved on 2026-11-02. Institution marks remain the property of their respective institutions. They identify the author's education and employment affiliations; no changes have been made to their designs or colors. Each row links to its institution's official website. The same assets are used in both language views.
