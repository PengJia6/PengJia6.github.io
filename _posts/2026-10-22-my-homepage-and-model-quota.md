I finally built my personal homepage today.

I had been meaning to do this for a while: a place to introduce myself, collect my publications and code, and occasionally write about research or whatever else comes to mind. It was always a good idea for another day.

Then I noticed that my AI usage quota was about to reset.

Suddenly, a project with no deadline had a very specific one. Rather than let the remaining quota go unused, I decided to put it to work. A few rounds of edits later, the homepage had a biography, a publication list, code links, and even this first blog post.

There were a few detours. My browser could reach GitHub while Git stubbornly refused to connect. The culprit turned out to be a proxy setting. Some things remain reassuringly familiar, even when an AI is helping you build a website.

Apparently, the missing ingredient was never a better plan. It was an expiring quota.

Not the grandest reason to launch a personal homepage, but it got me started. Now that it exists, I hope to keep it updated with research, useful code, and the occasional note like this one.
